Important technical facts
-------------------------
- The API validates access tokens using PyJWT and the Keycloak JWKS.
- It checks issuer, audience, expiration, and JWT signature.
- It extracts roles from realm_access.roles.
- It derives MFA status from the amr claim containing otp.
- Supervisor authorization is derived from the supervisor role, not a request field.
